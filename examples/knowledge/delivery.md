# Delivery

## Delivery charges
Delivery is free on orders over 499 rupees. Below that, it costs 40 rupees. ShopKart Plus members
get free delivery on every order.

## How long delivery takes
Most cities get orders in two to four days. Smaller towns can take up to seven days.
Same-day delivery is available in Bengaluru, Mumbai and Delhi for orders placed before noon.

## Changing the address
The delivery address can be changed in the app until the order is shipped. After that, it can't
be changed; the caller can refuse the delivery and order again.
