# Refunds

## When the money comes back
A refund starts once the returned item passes its check at the warehouse, usually two days after pickup.
UPI and ShopKart Wallet refunds arrive within a day. Cards and net banking take five to seven working days.

## Cash on delivery orders
For a cash on delivery order, the refund goes to the ShopKart Wallet, or to a bank account if the
caller adds one in the app before the return is picked up.

## A refund that hasn't arrived
If a card refund hasn't arrived after seven working days, the bank has it: the caller can ask their
bank with the refund reference number shown on the order page.
